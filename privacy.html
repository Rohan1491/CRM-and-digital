Privacy Policy

Arambhika Enablers · Last updated: 2026-08-11

This page explains what information Arambhika Enablers ("we", "us") collects when you contact us — including through Instagram (Direct Messages and comments) and WhatsApp — and how that information is used.

Information we collect

When you message us on Instagram or WhatsApp, or submit an enquiry through our website, we collect:

  • Your name, business name, phone number, and/or email address (when you share them)
  • Your Instagram user ID and username, or your WhatsApp phone number
  • The content of the messages, comments, or enquiries you send us
  • Notes our team adds about your enquiry (e.g. product interest, order requirements, follow-up status)

How we use your information

We use this information to:

  • Respond to your enquiries about our products (nickel strips, busbars, and related components)
  • Maintain a record of your enquiry and prior conversations, so our team has context on future contact
  • Generate a draft reply using an AI system (see below) — a member of our team always reviews and approves this draft before anything is sent to you. No reply is ever sent to you without a human reviewing it first.

Third-party services we use

We share the minimum information necessary with the following services in order to operate:

  • Anthropic (Claude API) — the text of your message, your prior conversation history with us, and our product catalog are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to help draft a reply. Anthropic's own privacy practices apply to this processing: anthropic.com/legal/privacy.
  • Meta Platforms, Inc. — Instagram messages and comments are received and sent via Meta's Instagram Graph API, and WhatsApp messages via Meta's WhatsApp Business API, subject to Meta's own data practices.

We do not sell your information to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Data retention

We retain your contact and conversation history for as long as it's useful for our business relationship with you, or until you ask us to delete it.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you what information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it at any time.

To make a request, or if you have questions about this policy, contact us:
Email: info@arambhika.com
Or message us directly on the Instagram or WhatsApp account you contacted us through.
